# Schedule 3 — Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 1.0 — 24 September 2026**

This policy forms part of the Master Services Agreement between Your Business Today Ltd ("**YBT**") and [Client legal name] (the "**Client**"). It applies to everyone who uses the Solution or the client portal on the Client's behalf. The Client makes sure each of its users knows about it before they are given access.

## 1. What users must not do

Users must not use the Solution or the portal to:

- upload, store or send anything unlawful, defamatory, or that infringes someone else's rights, including their intellectual property or privacy;
- store special category personal data (such as health, biometric or criminal offence data) unless the Order Form expressly allows it;
- try to gain access to any system, account or data they are not authorised to use, including any other client's;
- probe, scan or test the vulnerability of the Solution, or disrupt or overload it, without YBT's written permission;
- introduce malware, or anything designed to damage or interfere with a system;
- share their sign-in details, or pass an access token to anyone who should not have their access;
- give anyone outside the Client's named users access to the Solution or the portal;
- use the AI features to generate unlawful or harmful material, or rely on AI output for a decision that needs professional advice without checking it;
- copy, reverse engineer or resell the Solution, except as the Master Services Agreement allows.

## 2. What users must do

Users must:

- keep their sign-in details secret and use multi-factor authentication where it is offered;
- tell the Client's administrator and YBT straight away if they think their account, or the Solution, has been compromised;
- put into the Solution only data the Client is entitled to share with YBT.

## 3. If this policy is broken

3.1 YBT may suspend a user's access immediately where that is needed to protect the Solution, its data or other users, and will tell the Client's administrator promptly with the reason. Where it is reasonable, YBT will first give the Client the chance to put the matter right.

3.2 Suspending a user does not affect the Client's right to its data or YBT's duties under Schedule 1 and Schedule 4.

## 4. Changes

YBT may update this policy to reflect changes in the law or the Solution. Material changes are notified to the Client's administrator at least 30 days before they take effect.
